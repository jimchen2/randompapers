About consumer interfaces (like "https://chatgpt.com/" or "https://gemini.google.com/")

- LLM models cannot change their weights between different sessions to the best of my knowledge - they are all running on pretrained weights
- So if they "remember" you like in ChatGPT, they injected dirty stuff into the prompt - either through a hidden "memory" (where it sometimes self-prompt the answers) or through by using a RAG to search through your previous prompts first
- Anyway, I just use the API with my frontend: https://llm.jimchen.me/ Or you can use Nextchat https://nextchat.club/ or other pre-built frontend stuff
